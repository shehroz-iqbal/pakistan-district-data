- **F003** (t01_all, sindh, 12 rows): Same misspelling in the parent-district column of the tehsil-level Table 1 (district row and its three talukas).
- **F004** (t01, sindh, 3 rows): The Tando Muhammad Khan taluka (263 km2, population 272,427) is labelled as a second district row. Evidence: the district total 726,119 equals Bulri Shah Karim (247,027) + Tando Ghulam Hyder (206,665) + this row (272,427); and before the fix Sindh's districts sum to 272,427 more people and 263 km2 more area than the Sindh province row. Relabelled as a tehsil, so it leaves the district table.
- **F005** (t01_all, sindh, 3 rows): Same mislabelled taluka rows in the tehsil-level version of Table 1.
- **Table 13a** is not used for the 5-16 population: its conversion left 143 cells blank, every one a value above one million. Table 13b carries the same rows intact and agrees with 13a on every count they share.
